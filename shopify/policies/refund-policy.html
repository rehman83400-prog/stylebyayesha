14-Day Exchange & Return
You can request an exchange or return within 14 days of receiving your parcel. This applies to all items, including items bought on sale.

Conditions

  • The item must be unused, unwashed and unworn, with original tags and packaging.
  • Unstitched fabric must not be cut or stitched.
  • Please keep your order number or receipt as proof of purchase.

How to request an exchange or return

  1. Message us on WhatsApp at 0309 4746002 or email rehman83400@gmail.com with your order number and photos of the item.
  2. Once your request is approved, we will share the return address with you.
  3. Parcels sent back without an approved request cannot be accepted.

Return delivery charges
Return delivery is paid by the customer. If you received a damaged, defective or wrong item, we will cover the delivery cost and send you the correct item or a full refund.

Damaged, defective or wrong items
Please check your parcel when it arrives (you may open the parcel before paying the rider) and contact us within 48 hours if anything is wrong, so we can make it right.

Refunds
Because most orders are paid by Cash on Delivery, approved refunds are sent by bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa to the account you share with us. Refunds are processed within 7 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item.

Exchanges
You can exchange for a different size, colour or design of the same or higher value (you pay the price difference). Exchanges are subject to stock availability.
